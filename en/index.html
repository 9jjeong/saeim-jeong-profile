MECHANISM TO MEDICINE

Beyond mechanism,
toward evidence that reaches patients.

Sae Im Jeong is an Assistant Professor in the Department of Pharmacology at Ewha Womans University College of Medicine. Her research combines clinical study design with pharmacokinetic and pharmacodynamic analysis to determine how renal function, genetic variation, and combination therapy shape drug exposure and response.

Beginning with studies of aging, inflammation, the blood–brain barrier, and ischemic brain injury, her work has expanded into quantitative clinical pharmacology, sustained drug delivery, and clinical evaluation of wearable medical devices. A single aim connects the portfolio: enabling more precise therapeutic decisions.
